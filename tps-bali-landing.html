The fix

One platform that does the job of all of them.

Book any court on any system, find every open match, carry one level everywhere, and actually meet the people you play with. We don't compete with the platforms — we connect them. Here's how it feels.

01 / Booking

Every court in Bali, one tap to book.

Playtomic, Matchi, Taykus, Book & Go, Jungle Padel, Island Padel — pulled into a single feed. See real availability across the island and book without leaving the app.

02 / Open matches

The open-match feed that replaces the group chat.

Every open game, filtered to your level and your side of the island. Tap to join. No scrolling, no missed messages, no awkward "still 1 spot?".

03 / Universal level

One level that follows you everywhere.

Powered by World Padel Rating and approved by the federations — a single, honest number from 0 to 21 that every platform recognises.

04 / Community

The people, the events, the tournaments.

Socials, ladder nights and tournaments across Canggu, Seminyak and Uluwatu — built around players at your level, not a random invite link.
